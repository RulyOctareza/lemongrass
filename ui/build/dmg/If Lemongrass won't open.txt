If Lemongrass won't open
========================

Lemongrass is signed but not yet notarized by Apple, so the first time you
open it macOS says:

    "Lemongrass" Not Opened
    Apple could not verify "Lemongrass" is free of malware ...

Click Done (not Move to Trash), then pick ONE of these:

A. System Settings (no Terminal)
   1. Drag Lemongrass into the Applications folder and open it once.
   2. Click Done on the warning.
   3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
   4. Scroll down to "Lemongrass was blocked..." and click Open Anyway.
   5. Enter your password, then click Open Anyway again.

B. One Terminal command, after dragging the app into Applications
       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Lemongrass.app
   Then open Lemongrass as usual.

C. Next time, install or update with one command instead of the dmg.
   It downloads the latest release, checks it against SHA256SUMS and
   opens the app without the warning:
       curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/faizalv/lemongrass/main/scripts/install-mac.sh | bash

You only need to do this once per version. Source and releases:
https://github.com/faizalv/lemongrass
