macOS says “Lemongrass” Not Opened?

  1. Drag Lemongrass to Applications, open it, and click Done on the warning.
  2. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway.
  3. Or run in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Lemongrass.app
